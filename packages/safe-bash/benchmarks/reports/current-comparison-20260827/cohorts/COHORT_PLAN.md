## Mechanical overlap, not a universal denominator

byte-only input, full fixture metadata, script-plus-byte-input, and full
recipe/profile descriptor. It lists every matched ID pair, nonempty shared
payload hashes and shared lexical tokens. Profiles are separate identity labels
bound to their full manifest records. Equivalent bytes across different profile
labels do not establish equivalent execution conditions.

omit environment/configuration/profile. Lexical tokens include quoted text and
arguments; literal pipe/redirection character counts may include data and do not
prove shell syntax or invocation. Declared targets are provenance labels, not

meaningful independent coverage. Disjoint hashes do not prove semantic independence.

224,54,61,68,136 or24 into a universal union or pass denominator.
