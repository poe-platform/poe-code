## Review Boundary and Proof Roles

The future review target is the actual committed deferred executor, type-consumer
workers, materialized-import path and full-package guards, not a reviewer replica.
Root MUST first route their exact commits, handler/file paths, modes, hashes,
recipes and provenance, plus candidate binding and integration-owner acceptance
where applicable. Until then the state is PREPARED, not PASS or skipped-green.

Every obligation MUST declare SOURCE or RUNTIME proof and a separate job kind.
SOURCE means inspection of an authenticated committed handler; it cannot establish
execution. RUNTIME means captured execution of the declared path; synthetic Node
or fake-package execution proves framework behavior, not YQ semantics. DATA,
ADMISSION, SOURCE, TYPE, PACKAGE and INFRASTRUCTURE jobs MUST NOT enter a semantic
YQ numerator or denominator, even if they carry an original case ID. An actual
negative compiler result may be an expected TYPE payload only after its worker
verifies and records that result and exits zero. This never waives runner status.

The exact original ID set is selected from the five authenticated freeze files
and `MANIFEST.json#/coverage/groups`, not a computed registry or a sum of overlapping
194/80/62/32/64 cohorts. All 194 identities remain accounted for once, with their
eight accepted overlays; 194 is not automatically a semantic denominator.
The future complete job/role mapping and semantic subset MUST be declared before
execution and independently checked against the frozen obligations. Pending,
blocked, gaps and unavailable jobs remain visible and incomplete, never passes,
never dropped or rerouted to easier workloads. No full mapping is guessed here.

## Execution, Evidence and Failure Ordering

Future synthetic review limits are 5,000 ms per worker, 1,000 ms additional owned
cleanup grace, 120,000 ms total, serial fixture admission, at most four known owned
processes per fixture and 65,536 bytes per stdout/stderr stream. These are review
bounds, not product limits. Overflow or deadline is a visible failure with retained
bounded bytes and truncation metadata, not a silent pass. Production acceptance
needs its own authorized scope; this protocol grants none.

Before assertions can throw, the executor MUST preserve raw stdout/stderr bytes,
exit status and signal (including absence), effects, reasons and ordered events.
Reason representation MUST distinguish absent/undefined, null, false and stable
object identity where observed; diagnostics alone are insufficient. Capture or
assertion failure cannot erase earlier evidence. Receipt validation is subsequent
to capture: exactly one schema-valid, provenance-bound receipt per expected job,
matching run/job/candidate/recipe identity, with no unknown or duplicate jobs.
Missing, duplicate, malformed, forged or mismatched receipts cannot turn green.

Every nonzero runner child or signaled runner child makes the aggregate FAIL,
even with all PASS receipts or an allegedly expected negative test. A failed
semantic assertion may be recorded and independent cases may continue, but only
after both complete integrity and known-owned-child reap are proven. Overall
FAIL remains sticky. A later PASS never replaces earlier failure.

Integrity covers protected inputs, full candidate and recipe membership, bytes,
file types and modes, before execution and after every child, including failure.
New entries (including directories/symlinks), not merely original tracked paths,
MUST be detected. Mutation or uncertain integrity immediately stops remaining
admission. Guards are snapshots, not a lease or a claim to detect a mutation
restored between observations. Evidence outputs cannot be inside guarded trees.

The supervisor MUST enforce deadlines and prove reaping for all known owned
children before admitting another job. Only recorded owned PIDs/groups may be
signaled; foreign processes MUST NOT be searched for or killed. Parent exit alone
does not prove child reap. Deadline failure or unproven cleanup stops admission,
even if receipts say PASS. Opaque escaped-process limitations MUST be stated;
known escaped work cannot be declared reaped without evidence. There is no promise
of arbitrary process preemption or ownership discovery. Cleanup failures remain
recorded alongside the primary failure.

## Test and Validation Matrix

`FIXTURES.json#/controls` freezes observable negative controls and continuation
decisions. `proofRoleWitnesses` grounds role examples in original records and
accepted contract sections, without inventing executable methods or claiming a
full role partition. These are input scenarios and expected predicates, not runs.

Preparation validation checks JSON shape, unique control IDs, original-ID
membership, the exact overlay selection and immutable source hashes/modes.
It does not execute child processes, author code or historical checker programs.

## Conformance Criteria

Preparation is sealed by an atomic commit of exactly this protocol and manifest.

Later review remains INCOMPLETE until root supplies author seals; this reviewer
then authenticates and reads the actual handlers and consumers, maps these
predicates to their committed files/hashes, and executes bounded synthetic
negative controls against those paths. Binding failures, absent hooks or missing
coverage are reported as gaps, not solved with an invented executor or inherited
author results. SOURCE and RUNTIME findings, raw receipts, limits and remaining
gaps MUST be reported separately. No wait or polling loop is part of preparation.
