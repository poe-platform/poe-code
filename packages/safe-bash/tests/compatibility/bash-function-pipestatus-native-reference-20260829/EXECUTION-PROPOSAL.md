The nine source modules admitted from the accepted native12 preflight-v2 are
preserved byte-exactly under `inherited/materialized/*.mjs.data`. They remain
**inert historical source**, not a new runnable 26-case entrypoint or current GO.

1. `admission.mjs`: new profile/receipt schema, 26 explicit F/P identities instead
   of generated N01–N12; exact six-key environments and argv0; fixed source-fork
   reservations; explicit failed-lookup names in future GO; zero input fixtures.

   all tools/source pins, positional nonzero read, wrong FD/inode refusal, and
   no authority cycles. A profile rename is not permission to weaken checks.
2. `entry.mjs`: new unused root, managed ledger 27, preserve full retirement and
   final-deadline checks; accept only F05's exact bounded after-image; do not
   require every after-image equal before. Count declared source-fork reservations
   independently from the managed-process ledger. Remove N-only/zero-fork gates,
   not safety gates. No grant/receipt lookup may occur after launching Bash.
3. `observation.mjs`: host byte envelope rather than EREOBS1 parser; retain exact
   capture hash/status/effects checks, no semantic golden or output correction.

The other six modules (capture/lifecycle/state/storage/group-observer/
observer-state) are unchanged reuse candidates. Their old qualified results
apply only to exact bytes and old role assumptions. They do not automatically
qualify the three changed modules or pipelines/descendant-group retirement.

syntax/admission/observation controls remain required before independent review;
