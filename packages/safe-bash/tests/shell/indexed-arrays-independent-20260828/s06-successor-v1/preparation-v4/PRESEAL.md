## Fixed ownership and publication ordering

The controller inserts an ownership intent before calling spawn. The supervisor
stores the actual returned ChildProcess/pid immediately after spawn, before
observer hooks, waiting or publication. Events populate actual spawn/close/code/
signal fields; no fake close or pid is supplied when spawn throws/fails. Null
group absence means unobserved, not success. The most recent owner stays in the
controller even if any later hook, serialization, quota, publication or integrity
check throws. Original thrown reason identity, including false/undefined, remains
the child rejection. Facts are retained separately from optional receipts.

`cleanupReady` refuses active, unsettled or unknown-reap children. A synchronous
spawn-function throw is a recorded attempted launch with no returned child, not
a fabricated closed process. Actual ENOENT records its actual error/close events,
no pid and unknown/not-applicable group absence. Both remain admission failures.
Ordinary command status1 may aggregate after genuine close/group absence; a
receipt or lifecycle failure stops dependents. Publication failure is never a
successful receipt and cannot remove a child from accounting.

## One deadline, including final publication

The monotonic origin is process `performance.timeOrigin`, before initial
admission. The same6600000ms deadline is passed to the controller/supervisor;
controller construction does not reset it. All admission, storage, integrity,
cleanup, final-census and publication phases check that deadline. Pending final
publication/announcement promises race the remaining deadline and late rejections
are observed. Cleanup reserve remains120000ms inside, not after, the110 minutes.

FINAL is deliberately provisional. Its `eligibleAcceptance` cannot by itself
authorize a pass: terminal publication and zero coordinator exit must also occur
within the same deadline. A final write/announcement that completes too late
causes failure, even if bytes already reached disk/stdout. No finalization retry,
reset or new unbudgeted successful tail is allowed. This is cooperative deadline
enforcement, not a claim to preempt a blocked kernel or other synchronous host
primitive. Unknown cleanup or deadline overrun cannot become acceptance.
