# Stop Slop

Revise prose for directness and specificity.

- Cut filler, throat-clearing, and repeated framing.
- Replace vague claims with concrete actors and facts.
- Prefer active voice and varied sentence rhythm.
- Remove formulaic contrasts, inflated emphasis, and punchline endings.
- Keep necessary nuance; do not invent detail.
