## QBM-01 — CARRY, Before the Next Owned Unit

Let `c` be the invocation's pending ordinary owned units and `W = 1023`.
The accepted formula is unchanged:

```text
K(c, 0) = 0;                     c'(c, 0) = c
K(c, U) = floor((c + U - 1)/W);  c'(c, U) = c + U - K(c,U)W  for U > 0
total(c,U) = U + K(c,U)
```

All operands and intermediate arithmetic MUST be admitted before addition:
`c` is a safe integer in 0..1023; `U` is a nonnegative safe integer; for U>0
check `U <= MAX_SAFE_INTEGER-c` before c+U, and
`K <= MAX_SAFE_INTEGER-U` before U+K. Check the zero branch separately.
Invalid private arithmetic is existing internal misuse, not a new diagnostic.
The reservation also requires a valid bounded descriptor; an arbitrary safe U
is not authority to bypass existing node/value/collection/scalar admissions.

An ordinary unit with c=1023 MUST first pay/checkpoint, then do its work.
After exactly reaching 1023, `c` MUST carry across estimator/copy/encoder/other
owned phases and document boundaries. No arbitrary await or interpreter tick
resets it. Only a successful owned checkpoint resets the owned count.
`finish`, phase/document end and empty close MUST NOT flush or reset it.
If no owned unit follows, **no final tick is owed**. This is the versioned
selection of CARRY, not CLOSE-THRESHOLD or nonempty-only close.

Thus c=1022,U=1,remaining=1 admits cost 1 and leaves c'=1023. It owes no
terminal charge. A following reservation for U=1 requires cost 2; a following
ordinary charge must first pay its real checkpoint before the ordinary unit.
c=1023,U=0 costs 0 and leaves 1023 even through an empty finish. This private
zero-unit boundary is not a claim that a valid alias subtree has zero nodes.

## QB-F2 — Reserve Once, Copy on Prepaid Credit

The accepted proposal's six-step sequence is REQUIRED: resolve a completed
active anchor; separately charge a bounded async estimator; construct the
immutable descriptor; admit every logical projection; plan from **post-estimate
c**; call the one existing Budget.step(U+K) once, last among failing admissions,
before copy allocation. Then synchronously commit projections and install one
local reservation. Root's additional state/signal guards remain mandatory even
after that admission; they are not a new charged admission category.

U includes each copied node and ceil(payload bytes/1024) per distinct nonempty
key/string/canonical-number payload-copy operation. A bucket continues across
execution fragments of that same operation, but never merges different
operations. Estimation work/checkpoints are separate, not free or counted twice.
The immutable descriptor and composer-owned source cannot change before copy.

Every copy operation awaits `beforeUnit`. At a boundary it consumes prepaid
checkpoint credit and directly awaits the signal-bound immediate; it MUST NOT
call real Budget.step/tick again. Ordinary credit is consumed once before its
operation. The helper does not update or promise the engine's private nextYield
timing. No interleaved query/scanner/estimator/normal charge/nested reservation
may spend this Budget or alter c while the reservation is active.

Synchronous `finish()` requires exact zero ordinary/checkpoint credits and c'.
Overrun refuses before extra work; underrun refuses before publication.
`abandon` expires unused credits once, without refund or session reuse.
No terminal credit is added under CARRY. Caller-aborted admission does not
increment Budget; a live maxSteps failure may increment its private steps by
the attempted total before throwing, but installs no local projections/credits
and allocates no copy. There is no rollback, replacement Budget or post-failure
reuse. All descriptor and already-admitted output charges remain nonrefundable.

## QBM-02 — Mandatory Uncharged Guards

The borrowed signal and already-declared closed-admission state MUST be checked
**after EVERY await and BEFORE copy/allocation**, including a fulfilled
`beforeUnit()` which took no checkpoint at U=1. A final guard is REQUIRED
**before publishing any copy/result**, even after the last unit, exact credit
closeout, or an empty close. Guard failure permits no new allocation/publication.
Successful credits alone are not cancellation or session-state proof.

These are uncharged signal/state checks: no new tick, ordinary work unit,
diagnostic, error identity, public option or outcome precedence. They do not
reset c. A failed await's catch observes the same state while preserving the
already-declared caller-first / escaping failure / closed-session / cleanup
selection. Guard failures MUST NOT bypass finally or the admitted-work drain.
Closed acquisition admission prevents new work; it does not abandon required
cooperative cleanup of work already admitted.
During that required drain, state is still observed after awaits, but observing
the admission closure caused by normal close does not invent an execution error
or stop cleanup. Guards refuse new work/publication; cleanup observations feed
the already-declared outcome selection, not a new closed-session failure merely
because the invocation is closing normally.

The prepaid helper's before/after checks and rejected-await catch bind exactly
to the accepted clarification: if the borrowed signal is aborted, use its exact
reason, including false/null/undefined or an object; otherwise preserve the
original immediate rejection, even if cancellation-shaped or equal to another
reason value. Do not infer provenance from equality/truthiness. Only successful
await plus successful required guards permits checkpoint reset/ordinary work.
No publication occurs after abandon/close/cancellation. Already completed output
effects cannot be undone; root-caller abort observed before settlement still
wins under the existing cleanup barrier. Observe secondary cleanup failures.

Close remains synchronously registered before acquisition, shared/idempotent
with finally, admission-closing first, and draining registered cooperative work.
Stdout/stderr remain sibling scopes. Opaque host work receives no new ownership
or preemption. No guard is permission to replace a selected raw error with an
invented closed-session error or force every outer Shell outcome to reject.
