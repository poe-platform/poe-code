## Accepted profile and refusals

- Separate conditional-command AST; complete syntax parsing with lazy, once-only
  expansion of visited operands. No expanded-operator reparsing, IFS splitting or
  pathname globbing. Logical precedence and quote-fragment origin are preserved.
- Whole-string C-profile basic patterns for `=`, `==`, `!=`; limited signed64
  numeric literal/base comparisons, including empty0, not general arithmetic.
- VFS `-e`/`-a`/`-f`/`-d`/`-s`/`-L`/`-h`/`-r`/`-w`/`-x` follow the declared
  capability profile. Genuine `FsError` ENOENT/ENOTDIR/EACCES/EPERM map to false;
  unsupported/unobservable capabilities refuse2. Arbitrary errno-shaped objects,
  unexpected provider errors, caller/resource/sink/cleanup failures do not become
  false. Link-following exceptions remain `-L`/`-h`.
- `-v` supports scalar names/canonical numeric indexes only; `-o` reflects actual
  supported options. Reached aggregate `-v`, ERE `=~`, unsupported extglob/collating
  forms, timestamps and other held predicates are refusals, not GNU parity passes.
  There is no ERE Worker or new `BASH_REMATCH` behavior.
- Private4096-node/depth64 grammar admission uses `ShellSyntaxError`/status2,
  counting parsed branches before growth. Actual expansion/output resource limits,
  caller identity, sink failures and cleanup remain separate contracts. No new
  public limits or RSS/hard-preemption guarantee follows.
